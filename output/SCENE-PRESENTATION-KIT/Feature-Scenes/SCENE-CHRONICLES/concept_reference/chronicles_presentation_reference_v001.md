## Design Decision

The Chronicles are represented by a large open magical chronicle resting on a broad mechanical scriptorium table. The book is the dominant motif. Its blue-and-antique-gold diagrams connect celestial cycles, historical branches, places, nature, and event markers without using readable text.

The table is supported by one coherent oval architectural pedestal rather than individual legs. A restrained calendar mechanism, a Rune Dust vessel, a Dragon Relic chest, and a mechanical page-turning arm communicate time-based activities and rewards without turning the scene into a UI panel.

## Visual Locks

- One very large open chronicle book, centered and visually dominant.
- Delicate blue-and-antique-gold magical diagrams on both pages; no readable writing.
- Broad oval tabletop with an inset, subordinate calendar mechanism and small blue crystal.
- One centered stepped oval pedestal with a warm walnut core and warm cream limestone base and capital.
- Restrained antique-bronze bands and small slate-blue structural insets.
- Blue-glass Rune Dust vessel on the left and compact dragon-shaped Dragon Relic chest on the right.
- Small mechanical page-turning arm and one supporting parchment slip.
- Magic remains local to the vessel, page diagrams, and tabletop crystal; no tall free-floating magic plume.
- Stylized, readable 3D game-art forms on a warm ivory presentation background.

## Icon Derivation

Use the front view as the basis for a future game icon. Preserve the silhouette of the open book above the broad oval pedestal. Retain one central blue-gold page symbol and the small blue tabletop crystal. At small sizes, remove the page-turning arm, loose parchment, most diagram lines, secondary bronze brackets, and the two side reward props if they reduce legibility.

## Production Limits

- These images are concept views, not a mechanically exact 3D turntable.
- Small differences between views must be reconciled into one coherent model during production.
- No layered source, native scale, pivot, collision, topology, UV, animation rig, or in-engine validation is included.
- Runtime task text, progress values, timers, reward states, and interaction feedback must remain code-native.
