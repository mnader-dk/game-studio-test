## Design Decision

The Archive is a compact, freestanding magical library alcove. Its identity comes first from the broad, curved, two-level wall of densely filled bookshelves. The central open codex and the small knowledge lens provide the interaction and magic focus without displacing the library silhouette.

The scene communicates a read-only lore repository. It does not use card racks, card displays, maps, crafting equipment, deck-building language, ownership progress, or portal imagery.

## Visual Locks

- Broad, symmetrical, semicircular two-level bookshelf wall as the dominant silhouette.
- Warm chestnut timber shelving densely populated with grouped bound books and rolled scrolls.
- Warm cream limestone supports with restrained slate-blue panels and small brass diamond accents.
- Recessed upper gallery with a lightweight brass-and-timber rail.
- Central stone-and-timber lectern with one large, open, blank codex.
- Small circular blue-glass knowledge lens above the codex; it remains subordinate to the shelves.
- Blue-white and soft violet knowledge streams connect the codex, lens, and shelves.
- Two warm lanterns balance the cool magic and retain the welcoming library character.
- A small number of floating blank parchment slips may support motion and magic.
- Stylized, readable 3D game-art forms on a warm ivory presentation background.

## Icon Derivation

Use the front view as the basis for a future game icon. Preserve the nested silhouette of curved shelves around the open codex, with the small blue lens as the upper focal point. At small sizes, reduce individual books to grouped color blocks, remove the floating parchment slips, simplify the rail and floor pattern, and retain one strong magic stream between codex and lens.

## Production Limits

- These images are concept views, not a mechanically exact 3D turntable.
- Small differences between views must be reconciled into a single coherent model during production.
- No layered source, native scale, pivot, collision, topology, UV, or in-engine validation is included.
- Keep all magic local to the Archive object so it remains readable as a place of stored knowledge rather than a portal or elemental shrine.
