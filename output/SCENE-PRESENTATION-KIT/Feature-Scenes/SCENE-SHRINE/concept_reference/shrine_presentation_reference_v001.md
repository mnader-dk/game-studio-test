## Design Decision

The Shrine is represented by one compact freestanding ritual object rather than a complete room or
feature-screen composition. Its identity comes from a broad limestone arch, a circular stepped
plinth, restrained bronze construction, slate-blue inset stone, and a contained vertical source of
neutral crafting magic.

The presentation deliberately omits cards, Scrolls, faction marks, and canonical Rune glyphs. The
simple magical core keeps the object usable as a general Shrine destination without implying that
one Shrine action, affinity, card, or progression state owns the structure.

## Visual Locks

- Preserve the rounded pointed arch, open center, broad shoulders, three-step circular plinth, and
  centered basin as the stable silhouette.
- Preserve warm cream limestone, slate-blue inset stone, restrained antique bronze, and a neutral
  pale lilac-white magical core.
- Keep the magic visibly sourced from the basin, locally illuminating nearby surfaces, and separate
  from the structural object.
- Keep the central energy non-symbolic. It must not become a Fire, Air, Forest, faction, card,
  Scroll, or newly invented Rune glyph.
- Keep the stone, bronze, inner arch, basin, plinth channel, core energy, orbit streams, particles,
  and local light separable for future production and animation.
- Preserve readability without relying on the magic color alone; the arch and stepped base remain
  the primary identity cues.

## Icon Derivation

Use the front view as the starting silhouette for a future square icon. Retain the top cap, arch
opening, two side clasps, central energy core, and upper plinth. Simplify individual stone joints,
secondary particles, and rear steps before changing the outer silhouette. The icon requires its own
brief, native-size exports, background tests, and explicit approval; these concept renders are not
icon deliverables.

## Production Limits

- The three images are presentation references, not verified turntable renders or 3D orthographic
  projections; minor perspective and construction differences require resolution during modeling.
- No transparent layers, source geometry, pivots, masks, effect layers, or runtime package exist.
- The lilac-white magic is a neutral presentation choice and does not define a new affinity or the
  final Rune Dust identity.
- Native-size, grayscale, representative-background, responsive, animation, performance, and
  in-engine validation remain unassessed.
